# Research behind the egregore

Five surveys of how communities organize themselves, written to design Hive's egregore ([../egregore.md](../egregore.md)). Each one ends with ranked design principles and a numbered source list.

| Report | Covers |
|---|---|
| [biology.md](biology.md) | Slime molds, bacteria, social insects, flocks and herds, elephants, baboons, wolves, immune and neural systems |
| [societies.md](societies.md) | The Ottoman Empire in depth; Venice, Athens, Rome, the Haudenosaunee, the Mongols, the Inca and other polities; sociocracy, Holacracy, Toyota, open source and other modern self-organization |
| [fiction.md](fiction.md) | Heinlein's Martians and the Nest, Saturn Girl's Titan and the Legion of Super-Heroes, the Borg, the Formics, the Culture and other collective minds; the history of the word egregore |
| [theory.md](theory.md) | Cybernetics and complexity science, distributed systems, multi-agent systems, research on LLM agent teams, collective intelligence, organizational design |
| [communities.md](communities.md) | Wikipedia, Stack Exchange, Reddit and r/place, open-source governance, games, leaderless movements, religious and contemplative orders, crowds and markets |
